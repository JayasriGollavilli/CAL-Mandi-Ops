An Android app for fruit and vegetable commission agents and traders. It replaces the register, the calculator, the paper patti, the khata book and hand-written bills with one phone the munim can use while standing at the truck.

Interactive prototype. Every number you see is demo data calculated live on this device, and it works offline.

What the research changed

These decisions come from studying how existing mandi software, APMC yards and commission agents actually work, not from the original brief alone.

"PO" is treated as the arrival (aavak), not an ERP purchase order

Produce lands at the shop before any price exists. The app starts with the arrival and creates a lot; the price comes later.

Two ways to settle a lot

Commission sale: the patti is built from the real sale rates to different buyers, with a weighted average. Fixed-rate purchase: the trader buys at an agreed rate and resells. Established mandi packages support both, so the prototype does too.

Every charge knows who pays it

In Bengaluru's APMC yard, agents take their 5% commission from the buyer and charge farmers only for unloading. In other markets it comes out of the farmer's patti. One setting switches between the two.

Crates are money

Returnable crates are added to the buyer's crate balance automatically when a bill is made, and returns are one tap.

Perishable-aware alerts

The owner sees lots that have sat in the yard for two days or more, and buyers who owe money but have not come back for a week, which is the early sign of a bad debt.

No silent deletes

Bills and payments are cancelled with a reason, stay visible struck-through, and land in the audit log. Stock and khata reverse automatically.

Tax is off by default

Fresh fruit and vegetables are generally outside GST, so bills carry no tax unless the owner turns it on for a specific business need.

Questions to confirm with the client

  • Does the commission come out of the farmer's patti, get added to the buyer's bill, or both?
  • Is the rate usually quoted per kg or per crate for each commodity?
  • Are sales weighed again at the time of sale, or billed on the arrival weight per crate?
  • Do they give advances (dadni) to farmers before the season?
  • Is a crate deposit charged, or only a crate count kept?
  • Should the patti be given the same day or at the end of the week?
  • Rounding rule: nearest rupee, or down to the rupee?
